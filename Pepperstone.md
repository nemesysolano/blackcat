# Pepperstone Costs #

## Spreads ##
US Share and ETF CFDs has a fixed 0.02 USD spread per share.

## Trading Fees ##
Daily swap charge / credit = (market closing price x trade size x (our charge* +/- ARR) / 360.
*Our charge is 2.5%. Use a random ARR from a uniform distribution whose range muste a realistic one in market.

## cTrader Platform Fee ##
cTrader commissions are charged as $6 USD round-trip.
